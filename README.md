# 🛒 ShelfWise: Smart Pantry & Expiry Tracker

**Stop letting good inventory go bad—and stop losing money on expired stock.**

**ShelfWise** is a desktop inventory and point-of-sale application designed to track product shelf life, rescue near-expiry items with automated dynamic discounting, and handle customer checkouts cleanly. Think of it as an inventory manager with a built-in sense of urgency.

---

### ✨ Key Features

* **🚦 Smart Expiry Tracking:** Categorizes and monitors your menu/inventory items, clearly highlighting whether stock is **Fresh**, **Soon to Expire**, or **Expired**—so nothing gets forgotten in the back corner.
* **💸 Automated Rescue Discounts:** Automatically applies dynamic markdowns (**25%**, **30%**, or **40% off**) based on how close an item is to its expiration date, turning potential waste into quick sales.
* **🧾 Instant HTML Receipts:** Generates a clean, standalone **HTML receipt** for every completed purchase, ready to view or print.
* **🛍️ Smooth Cart & Billing:** Easily add items, delete unwanted entries on the fly, and calculate accurate final totals with discounts automatically factored in.
